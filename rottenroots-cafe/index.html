Our story

A small café with a lot of heart.

Rotten Roots Café started with a simple idea: make the kind of place we wanted to spend our own afternoons in. Good coffee, honest food, familiar faces and enough room to linger.

Our menu takes cues from Indian café culture and the things we grew up eating, with a few modern favourites we couldn't resist adding.
